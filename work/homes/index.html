How we do it

Home doors, done right the first time.

Your garage door is the biggest moving thing on your house, and on a lot of homes it's most of what you see from the street. We help you pick a door that fits the house and the way you use the garage, then hang it so it runs quiet and seals tight.

Most of the homes we work on want one of a few looks: raised panel for a classic front, carriage house with strap hinges and handles for a farmhouse feel, or a wood-look steel door that gives you the warmth of wood without the upkeep. Add a row of windows for light, and go insulated if you heat the garage or have a room above it.

For a replacement, we take down the old door, check the framing and seal around the opening, install new track, springs and the door, set up the opener, and haul the old door away.

What we install

  • Raised panel, long panel and carriage house styles
  • Wood-look steel in light oak and walnut tones
  • Insulated doors for heated garages and rooms above
  • Window rows, side stacks and decorative hardware
  • Single, double and multi-door garages
  • Old door removed and hauled away

Questions

Common questions

Should I get an insulated garage door?

If the garage is attached, heated, or has a room above it, yes. An insulated door holds heat better through a Wisconsin winter, is stiffer, and runs quieter. For an unheated detached garage, a non-insulated door can be a fine way to save money. We'll lay out both at the estimate.

Do you haul away the old door?

Yes. When we replace a door, we take the old door, track and springs with us.

Can I see what a door will look like before I buy it?

Try the Door Builder on our homepage to see styles, colors and windows on different siding. Send your build with your estimate request and we'll price that door.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We'll come out, measure, talk through options and give you a written price at no charge.
