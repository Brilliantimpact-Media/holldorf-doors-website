How we do it

Shop & commercial doors, done right the first time.

A shop or pole building door works harder than a house door. It's bigger, it opens more often, and it has to clear trucks, tractors, trailers and campers. We size the door, track and spring setup for the opening and for how much the door will cycle.

We install insulated commercial-style doors with or without a window row, residential-style panel doors on hobby shops and garages, and openers that can handle the extra weight. On new post-frame and steel buildings we work with the framed opening the builder leaves us.

Already have a door that's slow, drafty, off track, or still lifted by hand? We repair and re-spring existing doors and convert manual doors to automatic.

What we install

  • Insulated commercial-style doors
  • Tall doors for equipment, RVs and trailers

Questions

Common questions

What size door does my shop need?

Measure your largest piece of equipment, then add room on each side and on top. Most hobby shops land between 10 × 10 and 12 × 12, and equipment buildings often go 14 feet or taller. We'll help you size it before the building is framed if you call early.

Can you install doors on a new pole building?

Yes. We install in the framed opening on new post-frame and steel buildings. Getting us the opening size early helps us order the right door.

Can you put an opener on a big shop door?

Yes. Larger, heavier doors need an opener rated for them, and the springs have to be balanced right first. We'll match the opener to the door.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We'll come out, measure and give you a written price at no charge.
