What we do

Every part of the door, from the springs up.

One call covers it: picking a new door, hanging it, wiring the opener, or getting a stuck door back on track.

01

New door installation

Residential and commercial doors, sized and hung on new construction. Raised panel, carriage, flush modern, wood-look and full glass.

02

Door replacement

Tired, dented or drafty door? We pull the old one, haul it off, and swap in an insulated door that fits the house.

03

Spring & cable repair

Broken torsion and extension springs, frayed or snapped cables, worn rollers. Springs are under serious tension, so leave them to us.

04

Off-track & panel repair

Doors that hang crooked, jump the track, bind or scrape. We straighten track, replace damaged sections and get it running square.

05

Openers & conversions

Opener installs, repairs and replacements, keypads and remotes, and manual-to-automatic conversions for doors you still lift by hand.

06

Maintenance & emergency calls

Tune-ups that catch worn parts before they quit, and help when a door is stuck open, stuck shut, or the car is trapped inside.

Safety first

Torsion springs hold enough force to lift the whole door. Never loosen set screws, cut cables, or pry on a spring. That's our job.
